Acme Technologies is an IT services company founded in **2015** and
headquartered in **Bristol, United Kingdom**, with a second office in
**Porto, Portugal**.

As of the 2026 financial year Acme employs **248 people** and serves
**64 active client organisations**, predominantly in logistics,
healthcare administration and professional services.

## What Acme does

Acme runs managed IT for organisations too large for an office manager
to handle IT and too small to staff a full internal function. Three
service lines:

- **Managed Workplace** — endpoint management, identity, helpdesk.
- **Managed Infrastructure** — cloud estate, networking, backup.
- **Security Operations** — monitoring, incident response, compliance.
