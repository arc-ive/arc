# Acme Technologies — Benefits

*Synthetic demo document. Effective 1 April 2026.*

## Pension

Acme matches employee pension contributions up to **8% of salary**.
Employees are enrolled automatically after **3 months** of service and
may opt out at any time.

## Health

Private medical cover through **Brightside Health** is available to all
employees after probation, with the option to add partners and children
at the employee's cost. Cover includes **24 sessions of talking therapy
per year** at no charge.

Eye tests are paid annually, with a **£120** contribution toward glasses
where required for screen work.

## Learning

Each employee has a **£1,500 annual learning budget** and **5 paid days**
for study or conferences. Certification exam fees are paid in full and do
not count against the budget.

## Working pattern

Acme is **hybrid**: a minimum of **8 days per month** in an office, with
the rest remote. Teams choose their own anchor days.

Fully remote arrangements exist for a small number of roles and require
Head of Department approval.

## Other

- Cycle-to-work scheme
- **4 paid volunteering days** per year
- Enhanced company sick pay (see the Leave Policy)
- Interest-free travel season-ticket loan
