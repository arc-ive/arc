# Acme Technologies — Leave Policy

*Synthetic demo document. Version 3.1, effective 1 April 2026.*

## Annual leave

Every permanent employee receives **26 days** of paid annual leave per
year, in addition to UK public holidays. Leave accrues monthly and the
leave year matches the financial year (1 April to 31 March).

A maximum of **5 days** may be carried into the next leave year. Carried
days must be used by **30 June** or they are lost.

## Requesting leave

Leave is requested through the People portal. Requests of **5 days or
fewer** need your line manager's approval. Requests **longer than 10
consecutive days** additionally need Head of Department approval.

Notice expected:

| Length of absence | Notice |
|---|---|
| 1–2 days | 3 working days |
| 3–10 days | 2 weeks |
| More than 10 days | 6 weeks |

## Sick leave

Report sickness to your line manager before **09:30** on the first day.
Self-certification covers the first **7 calendar days**. Beyond that a
fit note from a GP is required.

Acme pays **full salary for up to 12 weeks** of certified sickness
absence in any rolling 12-month period.

## Parental leave

Birthing parents receive **26 weeks at full pay**, followed by up to 26
weeks at statutory pay. Non-birthing parents receive **8 weeks at full
pay**. Both are available from the first day of employment — there is no
qualifying period.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to **3 months** may be requested after two years of
service, at the discretion of the Head of People.
