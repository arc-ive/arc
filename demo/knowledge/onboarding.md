# Acme Technologies — Joining Acme

*Synthetic demo document. For new starters.*

## Before your first day

People Operations emails your start pack at least **one week** before
you start. Your laptop is shipped to arrive **two working days** before
your start date.

## Day one

- 09:30 — Welcome with People Operations
- 10:30 — Laptop setup, MFA enrolment, password manager
- 12:00 — Lunch with your team
- 14:00 — Security and data-handling induction (mandatory)
- 16:00 — First meeting with your line manager

The security induction is **mandatory for every employee** and must be
completed **within your first 5 working days**. Production access is not
granted until it is done.

## Your first month

You are assigned a **buddy** — someone outside your reporting line, to
ask the questions you would rather not ask your manager.

Probation is **3 months** for all roles, with a formal review at the
midpoint and at the end.

## Equipment

Standard issue is a 14-inch laptop, an external monitor, a keyboard and
a mouse. A **£300 home-working allowance** is available in your first
month for a desk and chair.

## Who to ask

| Need | Where |
|---|---|
| Laptop, access, software | Service Desk — `#servicedesk` |
| Pay, leave, benefits | People Operations — people@acme.example |
| Security question or concern | security@acme.example |
